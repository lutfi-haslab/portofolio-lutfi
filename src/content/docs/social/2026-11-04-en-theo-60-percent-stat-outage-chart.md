My favorite example this week of why claims need dissecting — even claims from people I trust, like Theo.

Theo posted that over two weeks, more than 60% of T3 Code users who used Codex also used Claude Code, and sold it as proof that the harness is the product. It sounds convincing, until a developer pulled data from both companies' official status pages for the same window.

The result: in those two weeks Codex sat under an open incident for 42.7 hours, while Claude Code had 34 minutes. People switched to Claude Code not because they fell in love with its harness; they switched because Codex was down.

My old lesson applies again: a stat from a product's owner about why you need the product always needs an independent check. That does not mean Theo lied — his data is real, the story is just different.
