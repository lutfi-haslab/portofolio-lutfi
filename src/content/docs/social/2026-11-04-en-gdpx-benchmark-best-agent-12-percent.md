Amid all the talk of ever-more-autonomous models, one new benchmark offers a number that is both calming and sobering.

SemiAnalysis launched GDPx: 206 long-horizon professional work tasks for AI agents. The current best model, Claude Opus 5.5, scores 12.0%. Not 90, not 50 — twelve percent.

To me this is not bad news, it is calibration. Short, well-scoped tasks are indeed largely solved, but long, messy, context-heavy professional work is far from done. Anyone selling agents today as full replacements for professional workers is selling a claim, not evidence.
