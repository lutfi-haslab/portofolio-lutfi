Today's company number comes from Anthropic's amended S-1, and both sides of it are extreme.

The growth side: Q3 2026 revenue hit $17 billion, up 1,127% year over year, with nine-month revenue at $33.24 billion. The risk side: forward compute purchase commitments of roughly $895.4 billion — bills that must be paid out of growth that may not continue. Quarter-over-quarter growth has already slowed from 144% to 47%.

I read documents like this the same way every time: revenue is evidence, commitments are promises. Anthropic is betting that AI demand grows faster than its compute bills. The IPO will show what price the market is willing to pay for that bet.
