Anthropic shipped Claude Meetings: Claude joins Google Meet as a visible participant, follows the conversation, and starts working while the meeting is still running — not after it ends with a summary.

What made me pause isn't the capability, it's the guardrail. It leaves on its own if an outside guest joins — but permission to record is now an admin decision, not the record button in the host's hands.

For me this is a question no product announcement can answer: once AI sits at the meeting table, our words stop being a conversation that vanishes when it ends, and become a record that is stored and quotable. Whether we want that is each team's call — one meeting at a time.
