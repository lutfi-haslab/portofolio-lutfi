A follow-up to yesterday's Kimi story, and this time the check is as simple as a calendar.

On October 23, Moonshot's technical blog described K3.1 Pro as a 3T-class open-weight model with 1M context, and promised the full weights would be released by November 4, 2026. Today is November 4, and as of this morning no K3.1 repository has appeared under the moonshotai organization on Hugging Face — the latest is still the K2 series.

Maybe the weights land tonight, maybe next week. But my rule stands: an open-weight model counts from the day its weights can be downloaded, not the day it is announced. Until the files exist, the status is still a promise.
